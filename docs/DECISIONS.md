# TradeQuest Decisions Log

## Accepted
- Platform: responsive web application.
- Audience: beginner individual learners, with community and challenges planned.
- Business model: free core experience.
- Progress: account-based for production; the initial prototype currently uses local browser storage only.
- Learning style: mixture of short text, diagrams, interactive checks, chart practice and feedback.
- Markets: forex, gold and other markets with instrument-specific specifications.
- Data: historical and clearly labelled synthetic datasets; no live market feeds in MVP.
- Simulation: educational paper trading only; no real-money execution.
- Content: AI-assisted drafting with automated checks and mandatory human editorial review.
- Product standard: crafted visual design, accessibility, educational quality and risk-first decisions.

## Must resolve before relevant milestone
- Historical provider and redistribution rights.
- Exact supported instruments/timeframes and instrument specifications.
- Cost/slippage assumptions and same-candle stop/target rule.
- Production auth and account persistence.
- Editorial reviewers and publication workflow.
- Community challenge rubric, reporting and moderation policy.
- Retention, deletion, analytics, hosting region, backups and incident ownership.
